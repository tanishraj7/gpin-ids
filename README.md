## Problem Definition and Focus
Though previous research has made great strides to the fields of intrusion detection and ML-based cybersecurity, an important void exists in bringing together local LLMs with on-the-fly packet dissection for attack detection and smart remediation. Notably, most explainable systems today either aren’t explainable or are heavily reliant on cloud-based LLMs, introducing latency, privacy and deployment issues in edge environments.
Our emphasis moves towards closing this gap with their GenAI-enabled Packet Inspector, which can not only detect threats through packet-level data with improved granularity but offer actionable feedback along with security improvement suggestions powered by a locally hosted, fine-tuned LLM. We have seen how this hybrid approach of lightweight anomaly detection models for real-time packet analysis leveraging the context-aware reasoning capabilities of LLMs can drive interactive and explainable cybersecurity.

## Justification and Relevance
Considering the increasing prevalence and complexity of cyber-attacks, particularly on the IoT and edge networks that are constrained by resources, combining intelligent local detection with explainable AI is crucial now more than ever. Our proposed system is thus a response to growing demand for autonomous, low-latency, explainable security agents, especially in domains where privacy-preserving and offline operation is necessary. To save public sector organizations time and money, the responsible use of GenAI improves understanding for users and supports smart decision-making, ultimately creating more intelligent, secure and intuitive systems.
This study seems to stack upon legacy methods iteratively developed over time and introduces a new, hybrid architecture combining the strengths of packet-level machine learning and LLM-based generative reasoning, referencing heavy recent successes observed in literature to support.
